# Getting help

## Using Sudocode

- Read the [User guide](docs/USER-GUIDE.md).
- Open the **Quick reference** inside the app for examples of each language.

## Questions

Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/humanspolluteearth/sudocode/discussions). No question is too basic.

## Something is broken

[Open a bug report](https://github.com/humanspolluteearth/sudocode/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml), or email [saysayeem.edu@gmail.com](mailto:saysayeem.edu@gmail.com). Please say:

1. What you did
2. What you expected
3. What happened
4. A screenshot, or the text of your program

## Sudocode and your syllabus disagree

Use the [language rule question](https://github.com/humanspolluteearth/sudocode/issues/new?template=language_rule_question.yml) form. Remember that mark schemes are the source of truth, not Sudocode.

## Security

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Please do not post security problems in public.
